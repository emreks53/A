# Kapanış görseli — Shutterstock yapay zekâ istemi (Yirminci Mektup)

Shutterstock'un AI görsel üreticisinde "Fotoğraf / Photo" tarzını ve dikey (9:16 ya da portre) oranı seçin, en yüksek
çözünürlükte üretin. İstemi aynen yapıştırın:

```
Ultra-realistic vertical photograph, 9:16, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.8, natural golden-hour light only, indistinguishable from a real photo. A deeply peaceful reading nook beside a large old wooden window at sunset. Through the window, softly out of focus: a calm lake surrounded by forested hills and a small town on the far shore, warm orange and rose sky reflected on still water. On a wide oak window sill covered with a plain cream linen cloth: in the foreground one hardcover book lies open and flat, facing the viewer as if we are reading it, cream pages with Turkish text. Behind it, four thick scholarly hardcover books stand upright, spines toward the camera, bound in matte leather of color #D68661 (soft dusty terracotta-rose), with gold-foil raised bands, a small Ottoman geometric ornament and gold spine titles "SÖZLER", "MEKTUBAT", "LEM'ALAR", "ŞUALAR". In the most honored place, lying flat and perfectly neat on top of two of these books, the Holy Qur'an bound in elegant deep ocean-blue leather, color #144173, front cover facing up toward the camera, with the Arabic calligraphy "القرآن الكريم" in graceful white thuluth script inside a delicate white Islamic geometric border with white corner ornaments. Nothing is placed on top of the Qur'an; it is not on any stand or holder. Warm sun rays across the books, long soft shadows, shallow depth of field, real leather grain, paper fibers and linen texture. No people, no hands, no candles, no lamps, no flowers, no extra text, no watermark.
```

Kısa sürüm (istem uzunluğu sınırlıysa):

```
Ultra-realistic vertical photo, golden-hour reading nook by a wooden window, calm lake and hills at sunset outside (blurred). On a linen-covered oak sill: an open hardcover book facing the viewer; four thick hardcover books upright, matte leather #D68661, gold spine titles SÖZLER, MEKTUBAT, LEM'ALAR, ŞUALAR; on top of two of them the Holy Qur'an lying flat, deep ocean-blue leather #144173, white Arabic calligraphy "القرآن الكريم" and white Islamic border ornaments. No stand, no people, no extra text. 35mm, f/2.8, real textures.
```

Seçerken bakılacaklar:
- Kitaplar birbirinin içine girmemiş, Kur'an hiçbir kitabın altında ya da arkasında değil, bir sehpa/rahle üstünde değil.
- Risale renkleri #D68661'e (soluk terrakota-gül), Kur'an #144173'e (okyanus mavisi) yakın; Kur'an'daki yazı ve
  işlemeler beyaz.
- Bir kitap açık ve bize dönük.
- Kur'an'ın üstündeki Arapça yazı bozuk çıkarsa yine gönderin: beyaz hattı kapağa ben doğru biçimde yerleştirebilirim.

Görseli (1–3 aday) bu sohbete ekleyin; videoya `kapanis/kareler/ai.jpg` olarak girer (yavaş kamera itmesi, kaynak yazısı
ve geçiş eklenir).

## GPT (ChatGPT görsel üretimi) için istem

Shutterstock'un sonucu beğenilmedi (Arapça yazı bozuk, Kur'an kitapların üstüne eğik dayanmış, sırt yazıları anlamsız,
örtü dağınık). GPT metni çok daha doğru yazar; aşağıdaki istemi aynen yapıştırın:

```
Create a vertical 9:16 photograph that looks exactly like a real camera photo (not CGI, not an illustration).

Scene: a calm, beautiful reading corner beside a large old wooden window at golden-hour sunset. Outside, softly out of focus: a still lake with forested hills and a small town along the far shore, warm orange-rose sky reflected in the water. The window and sky fill the upper part of the frame. Camera slightly elevated, about 30 degrees above the sill.

On a wide oak window sill covered with a smooth, neatly laid cream linen cloth (no messy wrinkles):
1. Foreground, bottom of the frame: one hardcover book lying open and flat toward the viewer, as if I am reading it; cream pages with neat, small Turkish text in a classic serif font, slightly out of focus.
2. Middle left: four thick hardcover books (about 5 cm thick each) standing upright, perfectly straight, side by side, spines toward the camera. Matte leather in exact color #D68661 (soft dusty terracotta-rose with a light-brown leather undertone). Each spine has two thin gold-foil raised bands near the top and bottom, a small gold Ottoman geometric rosette, and a gold title, exactly: "SÖZLER", "MEKTUBAT", "LEM'ALAR", "ŞUALAR", with "Bediüzzaman Said Nursî" in small letters near the bottom. No other letters.
3. Middle right, the most honored place, in the warm sunlight: the Holy Qur'an lying perfectly flat and straight on top of a neat stack of two more #D68661 books, front cover facing up and clearly visible. Cover: fine leather in exact color #144173 (elegant deep ocean blue). Centered on the cover, the Arabic words exactly "القرآن الكريم" in beautiful white thuluth calligraphy, correctly spelled, inside an elegant white Islamic geometric frame with white corner ornaments. All writing and ornaments on the Qur'an are white. Nothing is placed on top of the Qur'an, and it is not on any stand or holder.

Lighting and lens: low warm sun from the window, soft long shadows, gentle glow on the leather, 35mm lens at f/2.8 with shallow depth of field. Real textures: leather grain, slight wear on the gilt edges, paper fibers, linen weave, wood grain.

Do not include: people, hands, candles, lamps, flowers, extra objects, any other text, watermark.
```

Görseli ekran görüntüsü olarak değil, indirilen asıl dosya (en yüksek çözünürlük) olarak gönderin.

## GPT istemi — 2. sürüm (kullanıcının itirazlarına göre)

İtirazlar: fotoğraf gibi değil (mockup gibi), kitaplar RNK'ya benzemiyor, kalınlıklar gerçek değil, renk koyu ve dizilim
vitrin gibi, açık kitaptaki anlamsız yazılar seçiliyor, Kur'an'ın şekli ve konulduğu yer uygun değil.

Elinizde gerçek bir RNK kitabı varsa telefonla kapağının ve sırtının fotoğrafını çekip istemle birlikte GPT'ye ekleyin
(desen ve kalınlık için en güçlü yöntem). İstem:

```
Use the attached photos only as a design reference for the Risale-i Nur books (thickness, gilt patterns, spine layout); make them similar but not identical.

An unedited, real photograph taken with a full-frame camera (50mm lens, f/2.0), vertical 9:16. It must be indistinguishable from a real photo: natural colors, real textures, subtle real-life imperfections; no CGI, no 3D render, no product-mockup look, nothing perfectly symmetrical.

Place: a quiet, warm reading corner at an old wooden window at sunset. Through the glass, softly blurred: a calm lake, forested hills and a small Turkish town with a distant mosque on the far shore; the low sun reflects on the water. A wide wooden window seat with a soft, slightly creased natural linen cloth.

The Holy Qur'an has the most honored place: in the center, just in front of the window, standing upright on the sill and resting very slightly back against the window frame, front cover facing the camera, catching the warm sunset light. It is a real Turkish mushaf: portrait format about 17 x 24 cm and about 3.5 cm thick, rounded spine, fine leather in exact color #144173 (deep ocean blue, RGB 20,65,115), with a traditional fore-edge flap. On the cover, all in white: a central almond-shaped medallion with the Arabic calligraphy "القرآن الكريم" in elegant thuluth, correctly spelled; white corner pieces and a thin white border. Nothing touches or covers the Qur'an.

To the left of the Qur'an, three Risale-i Nur books stand upright side by side, slightly irregular like real books on a shelf: each about 24 cm tall and 17 cm wide, with different realistic spine thicknesses (about 5 cm, 4 cm and 4.5 cm). Bound in leather of exact color #D68661 (a light, soft, slightly faded terracotta-rose with a light tan leather undertone, RGB 214,134,97; not dark brown). Spines in a classic Turkish publisher style: several fine gold bands, small gold Ottoman illumination ornaments, the title in gold inside a small framed label: "SÖZLER", "MEKTUBAT", "LEM'ALAR", and "Bediüzzaman Said Nursî" in small gold letters near the bottom.

In the foreground, at the very bottom of the frame and strongly out of focus, one more #D68661 book lies open toward the viewer, as if I am reading it; its pages are so blurred that no letters can be read.

Light: low golden sun through the window, long soft shadows, gentle warm glow on the leather and gilt edges, a little dust in the air, natural lens vignetting, fine grain.

Do not include: people, hands, candles, lamps, flowers, extra objects, any readable text other than the titles above, watermark.
```

Kur'an'ı ortada pencereye dik dayalı değil de tek başına yatık istersen, Kur'an paragrafının ilk cümlesini şununla
değiştir: "The Holy Qur'an has the most honored place: alone in the center of the linen cloth, lying perfectly flat and
straight, front cover facing up, catching the warm sunset light."

Sonra aynı sohbette, gerekirse tek tek şu düzeltmeleri iste (her biri "keep everything else exactly the same" ile):
- Fotoğraf gibi değilse: "Make it look like an unedited real photo: add subtle wear on book corners and gilt, tiny dust, slightly uneven natural light, real leather grain; keep everything else exactly the same."
- Kalınlık yanlışsa: "Make the Risale books realistic: 24 cm tall, 17 cm wide, spines 5 cm, 4 cm and 4.5 cm thick; keep everything else exactly the same."
- Kur'an'ın şekli yanlışsa: "Make the Qur'an a real Turkish mushaf: portrait 17 x 24 cm, 3.5 cm thick, rounded spine, fore-edge flap, white almond medallion with correctly spelled القرآن الكريم; keep everything else exactly the same."
- Renk koyuysa: "Make the Risale leather lighter: exactly #D68661, soft faded terracotta-rose; keep everything else exactly the same."
- Açık kitaptaki yazı okunuyorsa: "Blur the open book's pages more so no letters can be read; keep everything else exactly the same."
