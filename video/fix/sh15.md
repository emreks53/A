## The client's absolute rule (this issue exists because of it)
NEVER a cross shape anywhere in the film - any '+' or 'X', even for one frame, INCLUDING lattices,
grids and line crossings. Two roughly straight linear features (bars, slats, their shadows or light
patches, edges, ornament strokes, creases, reflections) that cross each other at roughly 45-135 deg
and both continue past the crossing point form a cross; so does a four-way junction or a symmetric
four-arm arrangement of blobs/discs around a centre. T, L, Y junctions, V shapes and parallel lines
are fine. Judge at phone size (390 px wide) first, then at 100%; defocused shapes count if they read
as a cross at phone size.
